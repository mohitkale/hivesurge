# What retains players in crowd-runner games

## Benchmarks (already in master plan)

Mob Control D1 36.1%, D7 13.9%, D30 4.7% (AppMagic via [Udonis](https://www.blog.udonis.co/mobile-marketing/mobile-games/mob-control)); MAF cites D30 ~4.9%, D90 2.1% ([MAF](https://maf.ad/en/blog/mob-control-analysis-hybrid-casual/)).

## Level pacing & gate design

- Variety comes from obstacles, enemy difficulty, enemy types, gate multiplier numbers and small level objectives, giving "a light puzzle-solving feeling" ([Reverse Nerf](https://reversenerf.com/voodoos-breakthrough-with-mob-control-their-first-hybridcasual-success/), [Adrian Crook](https://adriancrook.com/how-to-build-engaging-economies-in-hybridcasual-games-lessons-from-crowd-city-and-mob-control/)).
- One thumb, portrait ("the holy grail"), playable while distracted ([Reverse Nerf]; [Naavik podcast](https://naavik.co/podcast/inside-voodoos-1-hit-the-story-of-mob-controls-200m-rise/)).
- Never require an ad to beat a level ([CrazyGames ad requirements](https://docs.crazygames.com/requirements/ads/)).
- Count Masters: short gate-choice runs ending in a battle; third-party review analysis flags heavy forced ads and thin progression as retention drags ([Marlvel](https://marlvel.ai/intel-report/games/count-masters-crowd-runner-3d), low-confidence AI-generated source).

## Meta progression

- Crowd City: only cosmetic skins + weekly mission leaderboard; no gameplay impact, faded ([Adrian Crook](https://adriancrook.com/how-to-build-engaging-economies-in-hybridcasual-games-lessons-from-crowd-city-and-mob-control/)).
- Mob Control: base + cards + league + pass give "short-, medium-, and long-term goals" that feel reachable; bricks scale with performance → "just one more battle" ([Reverse Nerf]).
- Built over 9+ months, iterating every 2–4 weeks against D30 and D120 retention ([PocketGamer.biz](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/mob-control-hybridising-a-hypercasual-hit/)).
- Risk: core and meta "stitched together artificially" ([Naavik](https://naavik.co/deep-dives/evolution-of-hybridcasual-deepdive/)).

## Live-ops

- Cheap, formulaic, mostly weekly events with minor rule tweaks (aim for one gate, collect items behind specific gates, loadout challenges, Piggy Race shared pot); unique rewards per event push multi-event participation ([Reverse Nerf]).
- Daily challenges: 3 mixed difficulty, refresh for 3 harder after completion ([Reverse Nerf]).

## Inference for Hive Surge

Ship the level-variety levers (gate values, moving gates, enemy types, mini-objectives) before more meta; one "next goal" progress bar on home; a weekly event that re-skins a level rule costs almost nothing.
