# M1 playtest: PR 002 (fix/pr-002-review @ e148dde)

Tester: hivesurge-qa. Browser: Chromium on the box, local dev server (vite :5180, worktree /workspace/hivesurge-wt/qa). Date: 2026-10-11.

## Verdict: PASS (desktop), with phone touch input still to be checked on a real device

## Checklist
1. Load: PASS. Battle HUD at 834 ms on desktop, PLAY at 477 ms on phone. No console errors, but 8 WebGL warnings (bug 3).
2. Complete levels: PASS on desktop with the mouse (won with 17 s left, 2 stars, peak 246). Phone touch is INCONCLUSIVE: emulated hold-drag did not fire. Input uses pointer events with `touch-action: none`, which should work, so this is likely the emulation tool. Tablet not run.
3. Fail: PASS. The defeat screen shows nest %, but see bug 2.
4. Revive/retry: PASS. Retry resets to 90 s/100%. Revive restores play once, and there is no second offer in the same battle.
5. Rewarded ads: PASS. The mock grants on completion and gives nothing on close. See bug 1 for the design question.
6. 2X: PASS. 75 to 150 coins, applied once.
7. Boosts: n/a.
8. Chests: n/a.
9. Mock purchases: PASS on the earlier build only (5173), not rerun on e148dde.
10. Interstitials: PASS. None after a win with 2X.
11. Feel: partial. Wasps are hard to tell apart at distance. Peak swarm was below 300.
12. Difficulty: borderline. 1 win in 4 attempts on e148dde, with a margin of about 15 to 20 s. No ad was needed to win.
13. Performance: NOT MEASURED. There was no FPS readout, and input was slow under tablet emulation (bug 5).
14. Screenshots: partial, see screenshots/m1/5180-*.

## Bugs (non-blocking)
1. Closing the revive ad without finishing it burns the battle's only revive. Repro: lose with nest at 50% or less, tap Revive, then tap "Close (no reward)". Design call.
2. The defeat headline always says "So close!", even at 100% nest. Repro: Play, don't fire, and wait for the loss.
3. three.js texImage3D FLIP_Y/PREMULTIPLY_ALPHA WebGL warnings (x8) about 20 s into the first battle.
4. Star row text is always ★★★, so screen readers don't hear the real rating.
5. Under 820x1180 device emulation, input took seconds to register. Needs a real-device perf check.
